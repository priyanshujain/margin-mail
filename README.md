# Margin Mail

A calm, keyboard-first mail client for Gmail on macOS, Linux, Windows and iPhone, with the parts of HEY and
Superhuman worth having and none of the tracking. New senders wait at the door until you let them
in; people, newsletters and receipts live in three separate boxes; what you owe and what you need
sit in two piles at the foot of the list. Every decision you make is yours, kept beside the mail
rather than inside Gmail, and it comes with you if you leave. It is a sibling to
[margin](https://github.com/priyanshujain/margin) and
[Margin Calendar](https://github.com/priyanshujain/margin-calendar) and shares their stack and
visual language.
