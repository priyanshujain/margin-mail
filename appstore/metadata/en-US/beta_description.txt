Margin Mail is a keyboard-first mail client for macOS with sender screening, separate places for people, newsletters and receipts, and Reply later and Set aside piles.

Test account connection, initial sync, reading and sending, sender routing, snooze, search, attachments and exports. Optional backup uses your own Google Drive or S3-compatible storage.

A Gmail or compatible IMAP/SMTP account is required. Some providers require app passwords. Proton Mail requires Proton Mail Bridge. Outlook sign-in is not supported in this version.
